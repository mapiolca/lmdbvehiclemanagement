## Couverture sans texte

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square 1024 × 1024 PNG cover illustration for the Dolistore listing of a Dolibarr fleet management module, in the user's existing family of blue pictogram-style covers.
Primary request: a polished, instantly readable blue illustration of managing a fleet of work vehicles and construction machinery.
Scene/backdrop: pure white, generous white margins.
Subject: a strong central three-quarter-view blue commercial panel van, with a smaller blue compact excavator behind on the right. Three carefully spaced secondary symbols: a maintenance wrench, a calendar with a single check mark, and a protective shield. All form ONE balanced compact composition, not a grid.
Style/medium: crisp professional flat digital illustration with subtle two-tone shading, clean thick contours and precise silhouettes, like a premium software marketplace product illustration. Palette deep cobalt #1455A5, royal blue #287CD6, very light blue #EAF3FA, minimal turquoise #80C5CD accents. Tiny restrained grounding shadow.
Composition/framing: centered, icon group occupies about 75% of the square; readable at thumbnail size.
Constraints: completely original artwork, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no legible license plate, no browser, no fake software screen, no photo textures, no 3D glossy cartoon, no background buildings, no watermark. The van and excavator are unmistakably distinct and well formed.

## Bannière française

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: wide landscape promotional banner, target 1792 × 1024, for the Dolistore product "Gestion des véhicules et engins".
Reference image: the provided blue van/excavator illustration is a style and subject reference. Keep the same cobalt/royal blue visual family and white background.
Primary request: design a compelling, extremely clean French software product banner with editorial typography, large white breathing space and the blue van/excavator group occupying the right-hand 45%. On the left, dark navy typography aligned flush-left with a generous margin. Do not simulate a software UI.
Text (verbatim), exactly in this hierarchy:
small eyebrow: "GESTION DES VÉHICULES ET ENGINS"
large hero, 3 lines: "Votre parc roule.
Vous gardez
le contrôle."
supporting sentence, 2 lines: "Affectations, consommations, échéances :
tout se retrouve dans Dolibarr."
small footer text: "Les Métiers du Bâtiment"
Style: sophisticated B2B software campaign, highly legible modern sans serif, calm confidence, geometric clean vector-like digital illustration. Very subtle pale blue circular background behind vehicle group. The illustration should show van + excavator and two small supporting calendar/shield symbols. Keep all wheels and excavation bucket inside the frame.
Constraints: correct French accents, exact text only; no extra text, no price, no numeric performance promise, no official approval badge, no version number, no logo belonging to another brand, no license plate characters, no screenshot, no chart, no watermark. Text and drawing must never overlap.

Référence de la bannière : illustration `illustrations/01-couverture-vehicules-engins.png` générée pendant cette tâche.
