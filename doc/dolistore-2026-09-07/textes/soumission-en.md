## Description longue

# Keep your fleet moving. Stay in control.

Scattered records slow your team down. **Vehicle and machinery management** brings your fleet into Dolibarr, connecting day-to-day operations, upcoming deadlines and supporting documents.

## Everything you need to keep track

- **Know who uses each vehicle:** assignments, usage periods and mileage history.
- **Understand consumption:** fuel, electric charging, additives, recorded costs and charts.
- **Plan ahead:** inspection schedules, insurance contracts, certificates and configurable reminders.
- **Connect maintenance to costs:** events, breakdowns and incidents linked to Dolibarr supplier invoices.
- **Build a vehicle dossier:** a PDF summary and ZIP containing eligible supporting documents.
- **Work in your ERP:** granular permissions, native attachments and Multicompany compatibility.

For construction companies, installers, maintenance teams and fleet managers who need information they can find and use.

**Give your fleet a central place in Dolibarr — from the first mileage reading to the vehicle dossier.**

## Before you start

- Dolibarr 20+, PHP 8.0+, MySQL/MariaDB. Module interface: French and English.
- Supplier Invoices and PHP ZipArchive are required for the vehicle dossier. Automated reminders require configured, enabled scheduled jobs.
- Inspection tracking uses a French regulatory catalogue; it does not perform inspections or produce official reports.
- PDF/ZIP dossiers exclude consumption receipts, their linked bank transaction receipts and GPS data.
